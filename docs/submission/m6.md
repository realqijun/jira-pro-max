# Milestone 6 - Monetization and pricing strategy

The current release is a free research preview. This removes friction while we validate whether the Evidence-to-Decision workflow solves a sufficiently painful problem. It is not presented as a completed paid subscription.

## Proposed pricing

| Tier       |     Indicative price | Intended customer                          | Scope                                                                                                |
| ---------- | -------------------: | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Preview    |                 Free | Individual evaluators and small Projects   | Core Project management, limited Evidence, Assistant quota and Decision Memory                       |
| Team       | US$12 per user/month | Small professional teams                   | Higher Assistant and extraction limits, shared templates, collaboration and analytics                |
| Business   | US$25 per user/month | Multiple teams                             | Advanced permissions, larger quotas, audit/export controls, integrations and priority support        |
| Enterprise |               Custom | Organizations with compliance requirements | SSO, retention controls, dedicated limits, procurement support and optional private model deployment |

Prices are hypotheses to test with early users, not claims about a finalized commercial plan.

## Why this model fits

- A per-user team subscription is familiar to project-management buyers and aligns revenue with the number of people receiving collaboration value.
- The free Preview supports onboarding and product-led adoption.
- Higher tiers can be justified by shared templates, governance, integrations, support and larger usage limits rather than by arbitrarily hiding core project data.
- LLM inference and file extraction costs scale with usage, so Assistant turns and high-volume proposal extraction should have fair-use quotas or usage-based overages.
- Users who bring their own provider credentials can reduce PrismPM's inference cost and give privacy-sensitive teams more control. Hosted models remain available for convenience.
- PostgreSQL, storage, analytics and model calls create a variable cost structure. We would monitor cost per active Project, tokens per Assistant turn, Evidence storage, extraction success rate and gross margin before finalizing limits.

Possible additional revenue streams include paid integrations, migration/import services, enterprise support and private deployment. We would avoid selling Evidence or project content because trust and data ownership are central to the product.
