# Milestone 1 - Competitors

## ClickUp

**Pros:** Broad project-management functionality, customizable views, automation, dashboards and increasingly capable AI features.

**Cons:** Its breadth can create configuration overhead. Project reasoning and source citations are not the primary organizing concept, so a user may still need to search across comments and documents to understand why a Decision was made.

## Asana

**Pros:** Polished task and workflow management, clear ownership, dependencies, workload views and strong adoption by teams.

**Cons:** It is optimized around work execution rather than a persistent, evidence-backed Decision Memory. AI assistance does not by itself guarantee that answers are traceable to approved project sources.

## Monday.com

**Pros:** Flexible boards, visual dashboards, custom workflows and accessible collaboration for many departments.

**Cons:** Flexibility can result in inconsistent project schemas. Important context may remain trapped in updates, files or separate boards instead of forming a connected history of Decisions and their consequences.

## Why PrismPM is different

PrismPM is not trying to win by having more generic views or more automation switches. Its differentiator is a **project memory layer**: Evidence can produce a proposed Decision, the Project Manager can edit and accept it, and the accepted Decision retains traceable citations, Assumptions, supersession relationships and downstream impact.

This makes the Assistant more reliable than a generic chatbot because it can answer “Why did we do this?” from a structured, project-scoped source of truth rather than inventing an explanation from an unstructured chat history.
